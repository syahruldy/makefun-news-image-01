# news-image-01 — Round 3: Friday 13 November 2026, 30 stories

A newspaper front page summing up Friday 13 November 2026: 30 stories
across 6 continents. Three hero cards (FAA ground stop, Iran radar
claim, Trump–Xi–Putin summit) lead eight smaller cards, and every
story is pinned on a world map under "WHERE IT HAPPENED".

The entrypoint `main.py` copies the bundled `art.jpg` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.
